Privacy policy

Last reviewed 9 August 2026.

Pre-launch note. This notice is complete apart from the registered company details, the retention period and the supervisory authority, each marked below in square brackets. Those get filled in before the site is submitted for indexing.

The short version. This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising scripts. It counts page views, and only once you have accepted the notice shown on a first visit. The only personal data reaching Intelligentside is what a visitor chooses to type into the contact form or send by email, and it gets used to reply, for nothing else.

3. What does not happen

  • No selling, renting or trading of personal data, in any circumstances
  • No advertising networks, no retargeting pixels, no social media trackers
  • No profiling and no automated decision making
  • No marketing list. An enquiry gets a reply, not a sequence of emails
  • No cookies set by this site, as set out in the cookie notice
  • No measurement at all until a visitor accepts it, and no tracking across other websites
  • No linking of page view data to an enquiry, or to any named person

4. Lawful basis

  • Steps before a contract. Where an enquiry concerns possible work, the data gets processed to take steps at the enquirer's request.
  • Legitimate interests. Answering a general question, keeping the site secure, and holding a record of correspondence. The interest is running a business, balanced against a visitor's reasonable expectation that a message sent gets read and answered.
  • Consent. Page view measurement, and nothing else, relies on consent. It happens only where a visitor accepts the notice, and withdrawing is as simple as clearing site data in the browser, which makes the notice ask again.

5. Who else sees it

Three categories of processor may hold this data, each acting only on instruction:

  • Hosting provider. Serves the site and keeps standard server logs
  • Email provider. Carries and stores messages sent to the address above
  • Form handler. Only where the contact form gets connected to one. While the form composes a message in your own mail app instead, no third party sees it at all
  • Measurement provider. Vercel Inc., which operates Vercel Web Analytics, and only for visitors who accept the measurement notice

Nothing gets passed to any other party except where the law requires it.
