What gets done

Seven things, one team, one invoice.

Booking

AI on WhatsApp and the phone

It answers every message and every call, quotes from the clinic’s own price list, handles the awkward questions, and books straight into the diary. It learns from each conversation, so the answers sharpen without anybody rewriting a script.

Diary

Calendars kept straight

Every booking written into the calendar the team already opens, with the client’s details attached. Confirmations, reminders and reschedules handled inside the same flow.

Social

Social accounts handled

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook run day to day. Posting, replies, comments and direct messages, all answered in the clinic’s own voice, with anything clinical passed to a person.

Creative

Posts made for you

A month of creative planned and produced: stills, short video, captions and tags, built around the treatments the clinic wants to sell rather than whatever is trending this week.

Paid

Meta ads managed

Facebook and Instagram campaigns built, tested and tuned. Spend gets reported against booked appointments, not against clicks and impressions.

Proof

Reviews after every visit

Once an appointment is marked attended, the same request goes to every client on the channel they already used. The clinic also gets a private line for anything that went wrong, so a problem reaches the team before it reaches anybody else.

Web

The website

A fast site built to turn a visitor into an enquiry, wired to the same booking assistant, and kept current as treatments and prices change.
