About

One loop, run properly.

Intelligentside is a marketing agency for aesthetics clinics, and it runs the whole loop rather than one slice of it: the ads that bring an enquiry, the AI that answers it and books it, and the review that brings the next one. Splitting those across three suppliers is where most clinics quietly lose money.

State of the art, defined

A claim that has to mean something.

State of the art gets used as decoration. Here it carries a plain meaning: the speech and language methods that hold up in production get applied properly, rather than a phone menu dressed up in newer language.

In practice that means:

  • Answers drawn from the organisation's own records, so the assistant reports what it found rather than inventing something plausible
  • A voice that copes with interruption, hesitation, and somebody changing their mind halfway through a sentence
  • Availability checked live at the moment of the call, never against a stale copy of the diary
  • A stated line for handing to a person, so the assistant knows what it should refuse to attempt
  • A review request that goes to every attended client, worded identically, because filtering who gets asked breaks the platforms’ own rules and corrodes trust besides
  • Method choices written down and open to challenge, never hidden behind the word intelligent

If a booking link and a tidier inbox solve the problem, that gets said. Putting a machine on the phone to patients is a real commitment, and it should only be taken on where the volume of enquiries justifies it.

Future proof, defined

Built so leaving stays possible.

A system is only future proof if walking away from it is cheap. Everything below is a standing commitment, written here so it can be held to.

  • Call recordings, transcripts and booking history export in full, at any time, with no charge for leaving
  • What the assistant says lives in readable configuration, not buried inside something nobody can inspect
  • Interfaces follow published standards, so a new calendar or messaging tool connects without a rewrite
  • No lock-in clause. The number, the data and the booking history belong to the business that paid for them
  • Written handover, so a change of supplier costs weeks and not years
  • Model and method choices documented, so the next engineer can read the reasoning

Straight dealing

What this site does not claim.

Plenty of sites lead with numbers nobody can check. This one holds a rule: nothing gets published here until evidence for it sits on file.

No borrowed credibility

No client logos, no testimonials and no case studies appear anywhere on this site. When a customer agrees in writing to be named, that changes, and the work gets described accurately.

No decorative statistics

No uptime percentage, no answer rate, no return on ad spend, no bookings recovered. Two things carry figures on this site: the prices, and whatever the diary model and the call flow compute while you watch them.

No invented history

No years in business, no headcount, no awards or accreditations. Intelligentside is early, and pretending otherwise would be a poor start to a working relationship.

No hidden pricing games

Scope, cost and the support window get written down before work starts. The business owns what it pays for, including the right to take it elsewhere.

Ask the awkward questions.

Method, cost, what happens if it does not work, and what leaving looks like. All of it answerable on a first call.
