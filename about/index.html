About

One job, done properly.

Intelligentside builds scheduling systems. Not project tooling with a calendar bolted on, and not a general software shop that will take a scheduling brief. Scheduling is the whole of the work.

State of the art, defined

A claim that has to mean something.

State of the art gets used as decoration. Here it carries a plain meaning: the scheduling methods published and proven in the field get applied properly, rather than a rota tool dressed up in new language.

In practice that means:

  • Constraint based models, so a rule stated once holds everywhere it applies
  • Solvers that rebuild a plan in milliseconds, which is what makes live replanning realistic
  • Every model measured against the plan the business already makes, on weeks already worked
  • A stated objective, so the system optimises the thing the business actually cares about
  • Method choices written down and open to challenge, never hidden behind the word intelligent

If a simpler tool solves the problem, that gets said. A scheduling system is a real commitment and it should only be taken on when the work justifies it.

Future proof, defined

Built so leaving stays possible.

A system is only future proof if walking away from it is cheap. Everything below is a standing commitment, written here so it can be held to.

  • Data stays in open formats and exports in full, at any time, with no charge for leaving
  • Scheduling rules live in readable configuration, not buried inside compiled code
  • Interfaces follow published standards, so a new tool connects without a rewrite
  • No lock-in clause. The logic and the data belong to the business that paid for them
  • Written handover, so a change of supplier costs weeks and not years
  • Method and model choices documented, so the next engineer can read the reasoning

Straight dealing

What this site does not claim.

Plenty of sites lead with numbers nobody can check. This one holds a rule: nothing gets published here until evidence for it sits on file.

No borrowed credibility

No client logos, no testimonials and no case studies appear anywhere on this site. When a customer agrees in writing to be named, that changes, and the work gets described accurately.

No decorative statistics

No uptime percentage, no efficiency gain, no hours saved. The only figures published are the ones the model on this site computes while you watch it.

No invented history

No years in business, no headcount, no awards or accreditations. Intelligentside is early, and pretending otherwise would be a poor start to a working relationship.

No hidden pricing games

Scope, cost and the support window get written down before work starts. The business owns what it pays for, including the right to take it elsewhere.

Ask the awkward questions.

Method, cost, what happens if it does not work, and what leaving looks like. All of it answerable on a first call.
